================================================================================
UNICODE-STYLE.EL (v0.42.4) — USER GUIDE & REFERENCE
================================================================================

1. INSTALLATION & SETUP
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Save `unicode-style.el` to your Emacs load path (e.g., `~/.emacs.d/lisp/`) and
add the following to your `init.el`:

  (add-to-list 'load-path "~/.emacs.d/lisp")
  (require 'unicode-style)
  (global-set-key (kbd "C-c u") 'unicode-style-prefix-map)
  (global-unicode-style-visual-mode 1)

Region commands operate on the active selection (highlight text first, then
press `C-c u <key>`). Live minor-mode toggles (`C-c u ,`, `C-c u v`, `C-c u V`)
work immediately without an active selection. Read-only buffers are protected.


2. SUPPORTED LANGUAGES (26 TOTAL)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
`unicode-style.el` preserves native diacritics, ligatures, non-decomposing
letters, and punctuation during styling and reversion (`C-c u a`), selects
locale-specific quotation marks (`C-c u '` and `C-c u ,`), and transliterates
everything to pure 7-bit ASCII in strict mode (`C-c u A`) for:

- All 24 Official EU Languages:
  Bulgarian, Croatian, Czech, Danish, Dutch, English, Estonian, Finnish,
  French, German, Greek, Hungarian, Irish, Italian, Latvian, Lithuanian,
  Maltese, Polish, Portuguese, Romanian, Slovak, Slovenian, Spanish, and
  Swedish.
- Additional Languages:
  Ukrainian and Russian.


3. ALPHABET & FONT STYLES

Mathematical Alphanumeric characters while preserving native accents and
non-Latin scripts.

  C-c u i   unicode-style-italic            Hello        𝐻𝑒𝑙𝑙𝑜
  C-c u I   unicode-style-bold-italic       Hello        𝑯𝒆𝒍𝒍𝒐
  C-c u j   unicode-style-sans-italic       Hello        𝘏𝘦𝘭𝘭𝘰
  C-c u J   unicode-style-sans-bold-italic  Hello        𝙃𝙚𝙡𝙡𝙤
  C-c u m   unicode-style-monospace         Hello 123    𝙷𝚎𝚕𝚕𝚘 𝟷𝟸𝟹
  C-c u c   unicode-style-script            Hello        ℋℯ𝓁𝓁ℴ

4. ENCLOSED STYLES (BUBBLE, SQUARE & KEYCAP)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Converts letters and digits into circled, squared, or emoji keycap characters.
Standalone two-digit numbers `10`..`20` convert automatically to `⑩`..`⑳` (in
`bubble` / `square`) and `⓫`..`⓴` (`11`..`20` in `bubble-black` / `square-black`).
Keycap mode (`C-c u k`) converts `0`..`9` to `0️⃣`..`9️⃣` and `10` to `🔟`.

  Key       Command                         Input        Output
  --------  ------------------------------  -----------  ---------------
  C-c u w   unicode-style-bubble            Abc 12 20    Ⓐⓑⓒ ⑫ ⑳
  C-c u W   unicode-style-bubble-black      Abc 12 20    🅐🅑🅒 ⓬ ⓴
  C-c u q   unicode-style-square            Abc 12 20    🄰🄱🄲 ⑫ ⑳
  C-c u Q   unicode-style-square-black      Abc 12 20    🅰🅱🅲 ⓬ ⓴
  C-c u k   unicode-style-keycap            0 5 10       0️⃣ 5️⃣ 🔟


5. SUPERSCRIPT & SUBSCRIPT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Maps letters, digits, `+`, `-`, `=`, `(`, and `)` into Unicode superscript or
subscript positions.

  Key       Command                         Input        Output
  --------  ------------------------------  -----------  -----------
  C-c u ^   unicode-style-superscript       (x+2)^2      ⁽ˣ⁺²⁾²
  C-c u _   unicode-style-subscript         H2O + x(i)   H₂O + ₓ₍ᵢ₎


6. QUOTATION MARKS (REGION TOGGLE & ON-THE-FLY MODE)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Converts straight ASCII `"` and `'` into locale-specific Unicode quotation
marks, and toggles them back to straight ASCII quotes:

  Key       Command                                  Action
  --------  ---------------------------------------  ---------------------------
  C-c u '   unicode-style-toggle-quotes              Toggle balanced `"` and `'`
                                                     in region to locale Unicode
                                                     quotes and back. Any odd
                                                     unbalanced quote at the end
                                                     is left untouched. Prefix
                                                     `C-u` prompts for language.
  C-c u ,   unicode-style-toggle-smart-quotes-mode   Toggle live minor mode that
                                                     converts typed `"` and `'`
                                                     into locale quotes on the
                                                     fly.

Selected Language Quote Styles (26 languages supported in `unicode-style-quote-styles`):
  - German (`de`, default fallback):  „Double“   ‚Single‘
  - English (`en`):                   “Double”   ‘Single’
  - French (`fr`):                    « Double » ‹ Single › (with NBSP)
  - Spanish / Italian / Greek:        «Double»   “Single”
  - Polish / Croatian:                „Double”   ‚Single’
  - Russian / Ukrainian:              «Double»   „Single“
  - Danish (`da`):                    »Double«   ›Single‹
  - Swedish / Finnish (`sv`, `fi`):   ”Double”   ’Single’
  - Hungarian (`hu`):                 „Double”   »Single«
  - Romanian (`ro`):                  „Double”   «Single»


7. LINES, UNDERLINES & CONTINUOUS DISPLAY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Applies combining line marks across letters, digits, punctuation, dashes, and
spaces while keeping any existing font style (bold, italic, gothic, etc.).

By default, `unicode-style-underline` uses `U+035F` (Combining Double Macron
Below) so underlines bridge smoothly across adjacent characters in external
apps (such as Signal), and automatically omits `U+035F` on the final character
so the line stops flush without overhanging. Single-character selections
automatically fall back to `U+0332`.

  Key       Command                         Input        Output
  --------  ------------------------------  -----------  -----------
  C-c u u   unicode-style-underline         Hello World  H͟e͟l͟l͟o͟ ͟W͟o͟r͟l͟d
  C-c u U   unicode-style-double-underline  Hello        H̳e̳l̳l̳o̳
  C-c u o   unicode-style-overline          Hello        H̅e̅l̅l̅o̅
  C-c u s   unicode-style-strikethrough     Hello        H̶e̶l̶l̶o̶

Continuous Display Controls in Emacs:
  Key       Command                                  Action
  --------  ---------------------------------------  ---------------------------
  C-c u v   global-unicode-style-visual-mode         Toggle continuous line
                                                     rendering across all
                                                     buffers.
  C-c u V   unicode-style-toggle-visual-size-limit   Toggle optional buffer-size
                                                     limit (default: unlimited;
                                                     toggles 500 KB cap).
  C-c u =   unicode-style-compose-underlines         Manually compose region.
  C-c u +   unicode-style-decompose-underlines       Show raw combining glyphs.


8. DIACRITICS & VECTOR ARROWS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Applies combining accents and arrows above letters (skipping spaces/digits).

  Key       Command                         Input        Output
  --------  ------------------------------  -----------  -----------
  C-c u "   unicode-style-umlaut            aou          äöü
  C-c u >   unicode-style-right-arrow       AB / v       A⃗B⃗ / v⃗
  C-c u <   unicode-style-left-arrow        AB / v       A⃖B⃖ / v⃖
  C-c u ~   unicode-style-left-right-arrow  AB / v       A⃡B⃡ / v⃡

Additional diacritics available via `C-c u RET` (`unicode-style-region`):
  `acute` (á), `grave` (à), `circumflex` (â), `tilde` (ã),
  `macron` (ā), `breve` (ă), `dot-above` (ż), `ring` (å),
  `double-acute` (ő), `caron` (č), `comma-below` (ș), `cedilla` (ç),
  `ogonek` (ą)


9. PUNCTUATION & PARENTHESES / BRACKETS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Key       Command                         Input        Output
  --------  ------------------------------  -----------  -----------
  C-c u p   unicode-style-fullwidth-punct   Hello, !?    Hello， ！？
  C-c u P   unicode-style-fancy-punct       Wait... !?   Wait… ‽
  C-c u (   unicode-style-brackets          (Note)   【Note】 〖1〗

`C-c u P` conversions:
  `...` -> `…`   |   `!?` / `?!` -> `‽`   |   `!!` -> `‼`   |   `??` -> `⁇`   |   `--` -> `—`

`C-c u (` bracket styles (selected via completion prompt):
  - fullwidth:   （ ） ［ ］ ｛ ｝
  - lenticular:  【 】 〖 〗
  - tortoise:    〔 〕 〘 〙
  - angle:       〈 〉 《 》
  - corner:      「 」 『 』
  - superscript: ⁽ ⁾
  - subscript:   ₍ ₎
  - ascii:       ( ) [ ] { }


10. COMBINING STYLES & REVERTING TO PLAIN TEXT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Key          Command                        Action
  -----------  -----------------------------  ----------------------------------
  C-c u RET    unicode-style-region           Prompt with completion for any
  (or SPC)                                    single or combined preset, e.g.:
                                              - `bold-underline`     (𝐛͟𝐨͟𝐥͟𝐝)
                                              - `italic-right-arrow` (𝑣⃗)
                                              - `gothic-overline`    (𝔤̅𝔬̅𝔱̅𝔥̅𝔦̅𝔠̅)
                                              - `quotes`             („…“)

  C-c u a      unicode-style-to-ascii         Revert styled text to plain text
                                              while preserving native spelling
                                              across all 26 supported languages.

  C-c u A      unicode-style-to-strict-ascii  Transliterate all Latin, Greek,
                                              and Cyrillic letters, quotes, and
                                              punctuation to pure 7-bit ASCII.

  C-c u r      unicode-style-toggle-cyrillic-transliteration
                                              Switch Cyrillic strict-ASCII mode
                                              between Ukrainian (`uk', default:
                                              И->Y, Г->H) and Bulgarian/Russian
                                              (`ru-bg': И->I, Г->G).


11. CUSTOMIZATION VARIABLES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Add any of these to your `init.el` to adjust default behavior:

  ;; Underline character: #x035F (Double Macron Below, default), #x0332, #x0331, #x0333
  (setq unicode-style-underline-char #x035F)

  ;; Trim U+035F on the last character so the underline stops flush (default: t)
  (setq unicode-style-trim-trailing-double-underline t)

  ;; Underline spaces between words (default: t)
  (setq unicode-style-underline-spaces t)

  ;; Render continuous lines via overlays inside Emacs (default: t)
  (setq unicode-style-continuous-display t)

  ;; Optional max buffer size in bytes for visual mode (nil = unlimited, default)
  (setq unicode-style-visual-max-buffer-size nil)
  (setq unicode-style-visual-size-limit-bytes 500000)

  ;; Smart quotation mark language ('auto or ISO code e.g. "de", "en", "fr")
  (setq unicode-style-quote-language 'auto)
  (setq unicode-style-default-quote-language "de")

  ;; Cyrillic strict-ASCII scheme: 'uk (Ukrainian, default) or 'ru-bg (Bulgarian/Russian)
  (setq unicode-style-cyrillic-transliteration 'uk)
